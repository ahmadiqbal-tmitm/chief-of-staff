Week 01 · Chief of staff

Your own AI chief of staff

Morning briefings, inbox sorting, meeting prep and a thought partner, inside Claude. It never sends an email or changes your calendar without your clear yes.

Want to build your own chief of staff? Follow the tutorial.

Step 1: Download the skill (1 minute)

Download the ZIP file. Don't unzip it.

Download the skill

chief-of-staff.zip · 9 KB

Step 2: Add the skill to Claude (2 minutes)

  1. Go to Customize > Skills.
  2. Click + Add, choose Upload a skill, and pick the ZIP file.
  3. Find chief-of-staff in your list and switch it on.

Step 3: Connect your email and calendar (5 minutes)

In Claude's settings, open Connectors and connect Gmail and Google Calendar. Sign in with the Google account you use every day.

Step 4: Create a Project (10 minutes)

Create a new Project called Chief of Staff. Inside it, start a chat and say:

Prompt
Set up my chief of staff

It asks you eight short questions about how you work, then writes your brief. Paste the brief into the Project's instructions, exactly as it tells you. That's what makes it remember you in every conversation.

Step 5: Use it every day

Inside the Project, say:

Prompt
Run my morning briefing

Other things to try:

  • "Run my end-of-day review"
  • "Help me prepare for my 2pm meeting"
  • "Plan my week"
  • "Help me think through a decision"

How it works

On its own
Read your calendar and email, summarise, draft replies, and move clearly promotional email to a Junk label (only if you allow it during setup).
Only with your clear yes
Send any email, accept or book meetings, change your calendar, create documents, unsubscribe, or delete anything. It shows you the full text of every email before it goes.
Never
Connect to your bank or payment accounts.

Expect the first week to be a little off. When something isn't right, tell it. It will give you a one-line fix to add to your brief. After a week or two it will feel like it knows you.

If something goes wrong

Skills is greyed out, or there's no upload option
Open Settings > Capabilities and turn on code execution. On a Team or Enterprise plan, ask whoever manages your Claude account to switch on skills.
The upload fails
Make sure you uploaded the ZIP as downloaded, without unzipping or renaming it.
It can't see your email or calendar
Disconnect and reconnect Gmail and Google Calendar in Connectors, using the right Google account.
It doesn't remember your setup
Check the brief is pasted into the Project's instructions, and that you're chatting inside the Project.
Briefings are too long
Tell it, for example: "Keep my briefing under one minute." Add the line it gives you to your brief.
