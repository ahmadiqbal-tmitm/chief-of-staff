# Chief of Staff agent

**Build your own AI chief of staff in about an hour. No code needed.**

👉 **Install it: [chiefofstaff.ahmads.build](https://chiefofstaff.ahmads.build)**

👉 **Build your own, step by step: [chiefofstaff.ahmads.build/tutorial](https://chiefofstaff.ahmads.build/tutorial/)**

## What it is

A personal assistant built as a Claude Project, connected to Gmail and Google Calendar. It:

- Gives you a **morning briefing**: today's meetings, the emails that need you, and what to do first
- Runs an **end-of-day review**: what you got done and what's first tomorrow
- **Sorts your inbox** and drafts replies for you to check
- **Protects your time** and flags projects that are slipping
- **Thinks with you** when you have a decision to make

It never sends, books or deletes anything without your yes.

## Who it's for

Anyone. The tutorial is written for people who have never built an agent: plain language, one action per step, and copy-paste prompts throughout.

## How to use it

1. Open the [tutorial](https://chiefofstaff.ahmads.build).
2. Fill in your week (Step 1).
3. Copy the instructions template into a new Claude Project and replace the blanks.
4. Connect Gmail and Google Calendar.
5. Say "Run my morning briefing."
